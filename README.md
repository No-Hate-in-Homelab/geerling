# No Hate in Homelab

An open, source-linked record of Jeff Geerling's public writing relevant to HomelabFest. The goal is to give the public, event participants, featured creators, and sponsors a shared source record, and to encourage Jeff to update or clarify statements as he sees fit.

## Current scope

The initial inventory covers public posts on Jeff Geerling's blog about LGBTQ+ issues and reproductive rights, the event's featured-creator list, its sponsor directory, and public statements relevant to the event. The sponsor directory lists DeskPi as Platinum, with Twingate and ServeTheHome listed as Other sponsors. Post entries link to the original and an Internet Archive search; a direct snapshot link is included where one has been verified.

This is a preliminary inventory, not a claim that it is exhaustive. Each entry should distinguish verbatim text, the author's framing, and this project's interpretation. Include relevant context and corrections, and update an entry if its source changes. Do not reproduce whole articles; use short excerpts where necessary and link to the source.

## Data

- `data/posts.json`: source posts and review status.
- `data/responses.json`: public statements from the event organizer relevant to the record.
- `data/creators.json`: names and public channel links listed on HomelabFest's featured creators page.
- `data/sponsors.json`: snapshot of the public sponsor listing.
- `data/organizations.json`: event organizations and contact channels published for organizational use.

## Contributing

Issues and pull requests are welcome for additions, updates, corrections, context, and archive links. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). Please include a direct source link and the date you checked it. Corrections are reviewed and noted in the change history.

## Privacy and contact boundaries

Only include contact channels that an organization publishes for event, press, vendor, or sponsor inquiries. Do not add attendee lists, private contact details, home addresses, or other personal information.
